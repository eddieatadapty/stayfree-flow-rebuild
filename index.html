Your ads × your flow

Three promises in your Meta ads. One paywall for all of them.

Your Meta videos ask “Looking for the park-ups like this?”, “Imagine finding a spot like this for tonight” and “Want to wake up here for free?”. Every install, whatever it saw, meets the same welcome, three setup screens, notifications, an account, then “Camp better with StayFree Premium”, before a single spot.

Flow Builder can open a different flow by the campaign an install came from, so the first screens keep that ad’s promise, and each ad is judged on the subscriptions it brings, not the installs.

Day 1

Pass the Meta campaign into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the campaign each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per promise

Park-ups, tonight and free each get their own first screens, in English and in German, since 8 of your 14 ads run in German.

Week 2

Judge each ad on what it earns

Each flow against today’s, measured on trials, paid subscriptions and what each install from that ad earns, so spend follows the promise that pays.

All three phones run the whole flow with the five ideas. A fourth promise has no phone of its own: “Would you like to camp here?”, van life in the south instead of a grey autumn at home, in English and German since 15 September 2026; it would reuse the “tonight” flow with the south pre-filtered, and it fits the season you are in now. We found no Apple Ads for StayFree. This layer carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.
